✦Why People Are Choosing Gold✦

Five Reasons to Own Gold & Silver Right Now

You worked a lifetime to build your retirement, and with the dollar slipping and the market swinging, it can feel like all of it is riding on one bet.

Here are the reasons people are moving a piece of their savings into physical gold and silver:

1

Inflation Quietly Robs You!

Every year, the dollar in your pocket buys a little less. What cost $100 in the year 2000 takes about $187 today, so a dollar you saved back then is worth about half as much now. Gold and silver cannot be printed, and over time they have tended to hold their purchasing power while the dollar slipped. (Source: U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Past performance is not a guarantee.)

2

Gold Is Yours. It’s Property!

It sits at the top of a very short list of assets that carry zero counterparty risk, which means no other party in the deal can default, delay payment, or fail to deliver on you. No bank or broker stands between you and what you own, and its market price can still rise and fall like anything else. It is also private: the money you move into gold and silver sits in your own name, outside the banking system, and is not tied to a bank or brokerage account number.

3

Gold Has Outrun the Stock Market!

The old thinking was to own gold before a bust, but years of money printing may be holding busts off. Either way, since the year 2000 gold has far outpaced the S&P 500, even after counting the dividends stocks pay. Ten thousand dollars in gold in 2000 would be worth more than $140,000 today, while the same $10,000 in the S&P 500, with every dividend reinvested, would be worth somewhere around $80,000 to $85,000. This window begins near a multi-decade low for gold, and past performance is not a guarantee.

4

As Liquid as a Phone Call!

How quickly could you reach your savings if they were tied up in a firm that closed its doors? Gold and silver turn back into cash with a phone call.

5

Streamlined Wealth Transfer!

Physical gold can pass to your heirs outside probate using common estate-planning tools: a revocable living trust, beneficiary designations on accounts like a Gold IRA, joint ownership, or gifting during your lifetime. These can bypass court oversight for a faster, more private, and more efficient handoff. Talk to an estate attorney about what fits your situation.
