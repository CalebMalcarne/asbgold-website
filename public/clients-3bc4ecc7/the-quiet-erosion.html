The Quiet Erosion

Why your money buys less every year, and what prudent savers are quietly doing about it.

Published June 2026 · 22 pages

Somewhere in your papers there is a statement with a number printed on it. That number is a kind of promise. It says: this is what forty years of work bought you. This report is about the reason that promise has been quietly shrinking, and about what a growing number of careful, unpanicked people are doing about it.

It takes about fifteen minutes to read. No hype, no doomsday predictions, and no manufactured deadline. It explains one thing carefully, so that when you finish you can make a calm and informed decision about your own money.
