01Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Mailing or physical address
  • Information related to inquiries, purchases, account setup, or service requests
  • Communications and correspondence with us (including calls, emails, and messages)
  • Any information you voluntarily provide through forms, phone calls, or direct communication

We may also collect limited technical data such as IP address, browser type, and website usage data for analytics and security purposes.

02How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Respond to inquiries and customer service requests
  • Process transactions and fulfill orders
  • Provide account support, including IRA-related services
  • Send updates, confirmations, and important service communications
  • Deliver customer support and follow-up communications
  • Improve our website, services, and user experience
  • Comply with legal and regulatory obligations

03SMS Communications

By providing your phone number and opting in, you consent to receive text messages from American Sovereign Bullion LLC regarding:

  • Inquiries and follow-ups
  • Order confirmations and updates
  • Appointment scheduling (if applicable)
  • Account or service-related notifications

Message frequency may vary. Message and data rates may apply. You may opt out at any time by replying STOP. Reply HELP for assistance.

We do not sell, rent, or share your mobile information with third parties for marketing purposes. Mobile information may be shared only with service providers necessary to deliver communications, such as messaging platforms, mobile carriers, and SMS providers.

04Consent to Communications

Consent to receive communications is obtained when:

  • You voluntarily submit your contact information through website forms, landing pages, or advertisements that include appropriate disclosure
  • You request information or services and provide contact details
  • You provide verbal consent during phone conversations

You are not required to consent to communications as a condition of purchase.

05How We Share Information

We may share your information only in the following circumstances:

  • With trusted service providers (e.g., payment processors, custodians, storage facilities, CRM systems, and communication platforms) necessary to operate our business
  • To comply with legal obligations, regulatory requirements, or lawful requests
  • To protect our rights, property, or safety, or that of our customers

We do not sell your personal information.

06Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse.

However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

07Data Retention

We retain your information only as long as necessary to:

  • Provide services and fulfill transactions
  • Maintain required business and financial records
  • Comply with legal and regulatory obligations

08Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal information
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your personal information (subject to legal obligations)
  • Opt out of certain communications

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the information below.

09Third-Party Services

We may use third-party platforms for services such as:

  • Payment processing

These third parties operate under their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their practices.

10Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. Your continued use of our services constitutes acceptance of any changes.
